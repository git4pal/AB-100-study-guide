# AB-100 Study Guide

Study notes for **Exam AB-100: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect**, part of the Microsoft Certified: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Expert certification.

This repository is a community study aid, not an official Microsoft publication or a complete substitute for the exam study guide. Exam objectives and product names change; use Microsoft's current materials as the source of truth.

## Exam at a Glance

- **Exam:** AB-100: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect
- **Passing score:** 700
- **Exam update:** Microsoft lists new skills measured effective **November 16, 2026**. This guide's objective outline follows that version.
- **Certification requirement:** The Expert certification also requires at least one qualifying Associate-level certification. See the [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/) for the current list.

### Objective Checklist

Use this as a progress tracker, not as a replacement for the detailed Microsoft study guide.

- [ ] **Plan:** Analyze requirements for AI-powered business solutions, including agent use cases, grounding-data quality, and organizing data for AI systems.
- [ ] **Plan:** Design an AI strategy, including adoption, platform and agent choices, knowledge sources, prompts, models, and business-app scenarios.
- [ ] **Plan:** Evaluate costs and benefits, ROI, total cost of ownership, and whether to build, buy, or extend AI components.
- [ ] **Design:** Design AI and agents for business solutions across Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry, Power Platform, Dynamics 365, and Microsoft 365.
- [ ] **Design:** Design extensibility, integrations, agent behaviors, and interoperability, including MCP where appropriate.
- [ ] **Design:** Orchestrate and configure prebuilt agents and AI features in Microsoft business applications.
- [ ] **Deploy:** Monitor, analyze, and tune agent and model performance; use telemetry and feedback.
- [ ] **Deploy:** Plan test strategies, metrics, and end-to-end validation for AI-powered business solutions.
- [ ] **Deploy:** Design ALM for agents, models, data, connectors, and AI-enabled business applications.
- [ ] **Deploy:** Apply responsible AI, security, governance, risk, and compliance controls, including access, data residency, auditability, and prompt-manipulation defenses.

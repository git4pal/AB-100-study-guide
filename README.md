# AB-100 Study Guide

Study notes for **Exam AB-100: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect**, part of the Microsoft Certified: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Expert certification.

This repository is a community study aid, not an official Microsoft publication or a complete substitute for the exam study guide. Exam objectives and product names change; use Microsoft's current materials as the source of truth.

## Exam at a Glance

- **Exam:** AB-100: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect
- **Passing score:** 700
- **Exam objectives:** Microsoft's next skills-measured update is effective **November 16, 2026**. The crosswalk below follows that upcoming version; check Microsoft's study guide for the version in effect on your exam date.
- **Certification requirement:** The Expert certification also requires at least one qualifying Associate-level certification. See the [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/) for the current list.

### Objective Checklist

Use this as a progress tracker, not as a replacement for the detailed [Microsoft study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-100).

- [ ] **Plan — Analyze requirements:** Assess agent use cases for task automation, analytics, and decisions; evaluate grounding data for accuracy, relevance, timeliness, cleanliness, and availability; organize business data for AI systems.
- [ ] **Plan — Design AI strategy:** Apply the Azure Cloud Adoption Framework; choose platforms, agent approaches, knowledge sources, and generative AI; plan multi-agent and prebuilt-agent use cases, solution rules, prompts, custom models, customized small language models, AI Center of Excellence, and solutions spanning Dynamics 365 apps.
- [ ] **Plan — Evaluate costs and benefits:** Define ROI criteria and TCO; analyze build, buy, or extend options; create a business-process ROI analysis; route requests to suitable models.
- [ ] **Design — Design AI and agents:** Select agent types and Foundry tools; design Copilot and agent experiences across Dynamics 365, Microsoft 365, Copilot Studio, and Power Platform; cover topics and fallback, agent flows, prompt actions, data processing and grounding, and AI-enabled business processes.
- [ ] **Design — Design extensibility:** Design custom-model solutions, Microsoft 365 Copilot agents, Copilot Studio extensibility and behaviors, MCP integrations, and agents that use Computer Use; consider Teams and SharePoint scenarios.
- [ ] **Design — Orchestrate prebuilt agents and apps:** Select and configure relevant Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 agents and Copilot features; consider Power Platform AI features and additional knowledge sources for Finance and Supply Chain apps.
- [ ] **Deploy — Monitor and tune:** Choose monitoring processes and tools; analyze usage backlog and feedback; interpret telemetry and performance metrics; use AI-assisted diagnosis and tuning.
- [ ] **Deploy — Manage testing:** Define agent test processes and metrics; validate custom models and prompt practices; design end-to-end tests across Dynamics 365 apps; use Copilot to create test cases.
- [ ] **Deploy — Design ALM:** Plan lifecycle management for AI data, Copilot Studio agents/connectors/actions, Foundry agents, custom models, and AI features in Dynamics 365.
- [ ] **Deploy — Apply responsible AI and security:** Secure and govern agents and models; assess vulnerabilities and prompt manipulation; validate responsible AI, data residency, data movement, access to grounding data and model tuning, and audit trails.

## Coverage Notes

The chapters provide a starting point for each exam domain, but are community notes and may not cover every product-specific objective or current feature. Compare each module with Microsoft's detailed skills list, especially for:

- **Plan:** prompt libraries and prompt-engineering techniques, customized small language model use cases, AI Center of Excellence, and multi-app Dynamics 365 solution planning.
- **Design:** Dynamics 365 Customer Experience and Service customizations and connectors; Contact Center channels; generative pages and agent feed; Power Apps canvas scenarios and Power Platform Well-Architected guidance; Computer Use, voice mode, and Microsoft 365 Copilot for Sales/Service configurations.
- **Orchestrate:** Finance and Supply Chain agent chat interoperability, customer experience and service capabilities, and adding knowledge sources to in-app help and guidance.
- **Deploy:** ALM for AI features in Dynamics 365 Customer Experience/Service and Finance/Supply Chain, plus explicit vulnerability analysis and mitigation.

Treat these as topics to verify and deepen—not an exhaustive list of omissions. Product names and exam objectives can change.
